Featured Project

Kenya Water Project Expansion

Nyamesocho, Kenya

Rianyabayo Memorial Academy

An award-winning pilot UV water treatment system, developed in partnership with Mitacs, Clear Inc., and MW Technologies, proved that safe drinking water can be delivered effectively. However, limited storage and system capacity mean the current system cannot meet full demand.

The Student Water Project aims to expand this proven solution to ensure reliable access to clean water for the entire school.

Virtual Running Fundraiser

12,000 KM Challenge

The 12,000 KM Challenge is a global running initiative. Participants run anywhere in the world and contribute their distance toward a collective goal of 12,000 km, representing the distance from Toronto to Kisii, Kenya.

Participants can:

  • Run outdoors or on a treadmill
  • Complete multiple runs throughout the day to accumulate mileage
  • Contribute any distance
